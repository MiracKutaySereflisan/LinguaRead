The old lighthouse stood quietly at the edge of the cliff. Every evening, its light turned slowly across the dark water, guiding small boats safely home. A young keeper named Mara lived there alone, reading books by candlelight and listening to the waves. She believed that every story, like every wave, eventually finds its shore. One night, a storm arrived without warning, and Mara had to climb the tower to keep the light burning. When morning came, the sea was calm again, and a single ship passed safely by, its sailors waving toward the small window where she stood.
